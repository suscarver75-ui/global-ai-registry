Registry Credentials

This area explains the registry credential process and keeps the portal usable while the full submission workflow is developed.

Credential registration

Online registration is not yet automated.

A visitor should not be told that a credential has been created until an actual submission, review, record creation, and publication process is operating.

The intended process is: submit information, review the submission, assign an approved registry identifier, create the registry record, and publish verification information where appropriate.
