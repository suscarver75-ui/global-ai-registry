Barefoot

👣 Movement is Life • Life is Movement

Program Foundation

A program that can grow in layers

Barefoot can develop into introductory learning, deeper studies, movement programs, spiritual/reflection tracks, community activities and educational resources. The program remains connected to the Holistic Health Department while having enough room to become a substantial destination of its own.

Safety & individual needs

Movement and barefoot practices are not appropriate in the same way for every person. Health conditions, injury, neuropathy, circulation concerns, balance, environment and other individual factors can matter. Educational material should encourage appropriate professional guidance when personal medical or safety decisions are involved.
