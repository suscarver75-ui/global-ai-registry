Research and healthcare notice

Do your own research and check with your own healthcare provider. This program provides education, cultural and historical reference, and optional wellness practices; it does not diagnose, treat, or replace licensed medical care. Never delay emergency services for herbs, energy work, or historical treatments. Historical accounts of tourniquets, wound cutting/cautery, poison treatment, rabies care and pediatric dosing are not instructions for modern use.
