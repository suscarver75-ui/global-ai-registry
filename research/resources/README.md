# LoAI Research Resources & Learning Library

Registry Anchor: LoAI-2024-0414-001
Department: Research

## Mission
Create an organized research-and-learning environment for approved LoAI research, authored materials, source collections, educational references, timelines, methods, technology research, ethics work and interdisciplinary learning.

## Collections
- LoAI authored research and working papers
- AI, technology and systems research
- ethics, consent, governance and human-oversight research
- education and curriculum support
- historical/timeline records and archived versions
- primary-source and authoritative external references
- datasets/data dictionaries where publication rights permit
- methods, protocols and reproducibility notes
- interdisciplinary research collections
- research glossaries and learning guides
- publications, presentations and approved supporting materials

## Resource record
Every cataloged item should support: title; creator; publication/original date; department; resource type; topic/tags; abstract/description; source URL or internal path; access class; rights/license; evidence/source type; verification/review date; version; related resources; and checksum/version history where appropriate.

## Evidence labels
Use plain labels such as Primary Source, Peer-Reviewed Research, Government/Institutional Source, Reference/Educational Source, LoAI Authored Research, Working Hypothesis, Conceptual/Theoretical, Observational, Experimental, Historical/Archive, or AI Synthesis. Labels describe provenance and should not be presented as proof of a claim by themselves.

## Learning pathway
Discovery -> source review -> compare evidence -> notes/synthesis -> verify important claims -> cite originals -> preserve version/history.

## AI integrity
AI-generated summaries and synthesis must be identifiable as synthesis and must not be presented as the original source. AI and technology can make mistakes, omit context or become outdated. Important information should be independently verified. Never manufacture citations.

## Access boundary
Only intentionally approved public resources belong in the public GitHub experience. Customer/member, department-private and owner-private research must remain behind appropriate access controls and must not be exposed merely because this catalog exists.

## Research notice
This library supports research, learning and education. Material may include hypotheses, historical records, developing work and conflicting evidence. Inclusion is not endorsement or independent validation. Readers should consult original sources and perform their own research.
