# LoAI Holistic Health Research & Resource Library

Registry Anchor: LoAI-2024-0414-001
Division: Holistic Health

## Purpose
Organize approved educational and research resources relating to wellness, holistic health, health technology, food/nutrition research, historical/traditional practices, ethics, privacy and healthcare-system resources without presenting the library as diagnosis or treatment.

## Collections
- health research and educational references
- wellness and prevention education
- nutrition and food research
- traditional, historical and cultural knowledge resources with respectful attribution/context
- herbs/botanicals research references
- mental-health education and support-resource references
- AI/health technology, interoperability, privacy and ethics
- healthcare provider and record-navigation educational resources
- public-health and government resources
- LoAI authored educational/research materials
- glossaries, learning guides and research timelines

## Evidence and provenance
Resources should identify author/organization, original source, date, last verification date, evidence/source type, topic, rights/license, version and related resources. Clearly distinguish established guidance, research findings, historical/traditional knowledge, hypotheses and AI synthesis.

## Permanent health boundary
This library is for research, education and informational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, cure or prevent disease and does not provide individualized medical advice. Users should do their own research, verify important information with original/authoritative sources, and discuss symptoms, diagnoses, medications, supplements, treatments or changes in care with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

AI and technology can make mistakes, misunderstand context, omit information or rely on sources that later change. AI output is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment.

## Higher-risk content
Content involving emergencies, serious symptoms, medications/interactions, pregnancy, children, mental-health crises, major medical conditions or other higher-risk subjects should display stronger context-specific safety guidance and direct users to appropriate qualified or emergency services when warranted.

## Access boundary
Public GitHub contains only intentionally approved public educational resources and catalog structure. Personal health information, private records, private research, member/customer records and owner-only material must not be committed here.
