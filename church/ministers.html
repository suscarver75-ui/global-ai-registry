OUR APPROACH

Ancient responsibilities. Modern access.

Holy Persons, Medicine People, plant knowledge, spiritual leadership and relationships with Earth are not modern inventions. Indigenous peoples around the world have carried distinct systems of spiritual, ecological and community knowledge across generations. What is modern here is the church's use of digital learning, research tools and responsible AI to help organize resources, support study and connect people with human ministry.

Seven-Fold is spiritual and Earth-centered rather than limited to one denomination. Christian faith and Bible study have an important home here alongside Indigenous, ancestral, Earth-based and comparative spiritual learning.

MINISTRY PATH

Holy Person Minister

Spiritual and ceremonial leadership: prayer, sacred words, song and chant, drum, fire, water, smoke, plants, sacred space, ceremony sequence, spiritual listening, life transitions, community care, consent and safeguarding.

Primary role: learning how spiritual elements connect, when they are appropriate, and when they should not be used.

MINISTRY PATH

Plant Minister

Earth and ecological knowledge: plants, herbs, roots, trees, seeds, soil, minerals, water, ecosystems, seasons, identification, cultivation, harvesting, preservation, foodways, ethnobotany, conservation and safety research.

Primary role: serving as an educational Earth-knowledge resource and supporting ministry when plant or environmental knowledge is needed.

Two roles that can work together

The paths are different but intentionally overlap. A Holy Person Minister may lead an approved spiritual service while a Plant Minister supports plant identification, sourcing, ecological knowledge and safe handling. A qualified person may complete both church training paths.

Shared Foundation

Ethics, spiritual care, cultural respect, consent, safeguarding, Earth stewardship, emergency recognition, research literacy and responsible technology.

Specialized Training

Holy Person Ministry, Plant Ministry, Christian ministry leadership and additional future ministry studies.

Guided Practice

Knowledge is paired with supervised learning, demonstrated responsibility and church review.

Continuing Learning

The Academy and Library continue growing with research, cultural material and safety updates.

Permanent ministry boundary: Holy Person Ministers, Plant Ministers and church AI provide spiritual ministry, education, historical and cultural study, and informational resources. They do not diagnose disease, prescribe medical treatment or represent a ministry title as a healthcare license.

The working manual is now open
