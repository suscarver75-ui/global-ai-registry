OUR APPROACH

Ancient responsibilities. Modern access.

Holy Persons, Medicine People, plant knowledge, spiritual leadership and relationships with Earth are not modern inventions. Indigenous peoples around the world have carried distinct systems of spiritual, ecological and community knowledge across generations. What is modern here is the church's use of digital learning, research tools and responsible AI to help organize resources, support study and connect people with human ministry.

Seven-Fold is spiritual and Earth-centered rather than limited to one denomination. Christian faith and Bible study have an important home here alongside Indigenous, ancestral, Earth-based and comparative spiritual learning. Distinct peoples and traditions are respected as distinct.

MINISTRY PATH

Holy Person Minister

The Holy Person Minister path prepares spiritual and ceremonial leaders to understand how the elements of ministry connect and when they are appropriate.

  • Prayer, sacred and devotional words, song and chant
  • Drum, rhythm, silence and community participation
  • Fire, water, smoke, sacred space and ceremonial preparation
  • Plants and herbs within appropriate spiritual contexts
  • Timing, amounts, sequence, boundaries and participant safety
  • Spiritual listening, grief support, life transitions and community care
  • Christian, Indigenous, Earth-based and comparative spiritual study
  • Consent, cultural responsibility, safeguarding and referral

Primary role: spiritual leadership and approved church ceremony. The minister learns not only individual elements, but how—and when—not—to bring them together.

MINISTRY PATH

Plant Minister

The Plant Minister path develops deep working knowledge of the living environment and becomes an educational resource for members and ministry teams.

  • Plant, herb, tree, root and seed identification
  • Soil, minerals, water, watersheds and ecological conditions
  • Growth cycles, seasons and responsible harvesting
  • Cultivation, seed saving, storage and preservation
  • Foodways, ethnobotany and historical plant traditions
  • Dangerous look-alikes, toxicity and safety research
  • Stewardship, conservation and respect for the land
  • Research skills and reliable resource navigation

Primary role: plant and Earth knowledge, education and stewardship. A Plant Minister may strongly assist a Holy Person Minister when plant or environmental knowledge is needed.

Two roles that can work together

The paths are different, but they intentionally overlap. In ceremonies or community work, a Holy Person Minister may lead the spiritual structure while a Plant Minister supports plant identification, sourcing, environmental knowledge and safe handling. In smaller settings one qualified person may eventually complete both church training paths and carry both sets of responsibilities.

Shared Foundation

Ethics, spiritual care, cultural respect, consent, safeguarding, Earth stewardship, emergency recognition, research literacy and responsible technology.

Specialized Training

Students branch into Holy Person Ministry, Plant Ministry, Christian ministry leadership and additional future ministry studies.

Guided Practice

Knowledge is paired with supervised learning, demonstrated responsibility and church review before advanced ministry responsibilities are recognized.

Continuing Learning

Ministers continue studying. The Academy and Library grow with historical sources, cultural material, research, safety updates and new educational resources.

Ministry Academy curriculum framework

Spiritual Foundations

Creator and sacred understandings, prayer, spiritual diversity, Christian studies, Indigenous worldviews, Earth-centered spirituality and comparative study.

Ceremony & Leadership

Preparation, sacred space, song, chant, drum, fire, water, smoke, plants, community circles, weddings, blessings, memorials and other approved services.

Earth & Plant Knowledge

Botany, herbs, roots, trees, soil, water, minerals, seasons, harvesting, cultivation, preservation, ecology and stewardship.

Community Care

Listening, prayer support, grief, recovery support, family and community ministry, boundaries, crisis recognition and appropriate referral.

History & Heritage

Global Indigenous peoples, cultural histories, Christian history, folk traditions, material culture, Powwow/Braucherei studies and intergenerational knowledge.

Technology & Research

Using the digital library, evaluating sources, preserving provenance, AI-assisted research, privacy, records and responsible digital ministry.

Permanent ministry boundary: Holy Person Ministers, Plant Ministers and church AI provide spiritual ministry, education, historical and cultural study, and informational resources. They do not diagnose disease, prescribe medical treatment, or use a church title as a substitute for a licensed healthcare credential. Members may study traditional plant and healing knowledge with safety information and appropriate professional referrals.

Training, recognition & service

Interest begins with ministry intake and does not itself confer a title. The developing process includes application, foundational study, specialized curriculum, practical formation, ethics and safety preparation, church review, and continuing education. Additional pathways—including Christian ministry, chaplaincy, Bible study leadership and future specialized ministries—will be integrated into the Academy rather than forcing every person into one spiritual path.
