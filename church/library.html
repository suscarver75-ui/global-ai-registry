Knowledge Provenance

Not all knowledge comes from the same kind of source.

The Library distinguishes historical documents, scholarly research, Indigenous/community knowledge, family and intergenerational knowledge, practitioner knowledge, oral history and modern interpretation. Resources can be connected without pretending every source has the same origin or purpose.
