Knowledge Provenance

Not all knowledge comes from the same kind of source.

The Library is designed to distinguish historical documents, scholarly research, Indigenous/community knowledge, family and intergenerational knowledge, practitioner knowledge, material/technical knowledge, oral history and modern interpretation. Resources can be connected without pretending every source has the same origin or purpose.

As the digital system grows, records can carry source, culture/community, public or restricted status, historical or contemporary context, safety review, references and review dates so knowledge does not become separated from where it came from.
