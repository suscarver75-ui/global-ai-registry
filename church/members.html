Community Foundation

Many peoples. Many traditions. Shared responsibility.

We are a spiritual and Earth-centered community with Indigenous inspiration and global respect. Christian faith and Bible study have a meaningful home here alongside Indigenous, ancestral, Earth-based and comparative spiritual learning. God, Creator and the sacred are understood through many names and traditions; participation is not limited to one denomination.

We respect Indigenous peoples as distinct peoples with distinct histories, teachings and traditions. We welcome members of every race and cultural background and do not treat one people's traditions as a replacement for another's.

🤝

Membership

Membership opens a path into spiritual learning, community participation, services, study groups, volunteering and other church opportunities as they become available.

  • Respect the dignity and spiritual freedom of others
  • Follow community conduct and safety standards
  • Protect private and culturally sensitive information
  • Participate without discrimination or harassment
🙏

Spiritual Life

Members can participate in prayer, reflection, services, Bible study, Earth-centered learning, cultural and historical studies, and approved spiritual gatherings without being required to follow every spiritual path represented in the Library.

📚

Education

The Library and School of Continuing Education provide research, study resources, featured studies, ministry education and growing collections on faith, heritage, plants, Earth knowledge, history and technology.

🕊️

Service & Ministry

Members who feel called to deeper service can explore ministry training. Ministry roles require additional preparation, review and responsibility beyond ordinary membership.

🌎

Global Respect

The church uplifts Native American heritage while also respecting Indigenous peoples and tribes throughout the world. Cultural material should be identified by its people, place and source whenever that information is known.

🤖

Human + Technology

AI and digital systems help members find resources, organize learning and connect information. Technology supports the community; it does not replace human spiritual leadership, consent, cultural authority or personal responsibility.

Children & Families

All ages can learn—with appropriate adult protection.

Family and youth education can be part of the church community. Children and minors must participate under appropriate parent or legal-guardian oversight. Private communication, accounts, membership features, events and ministry activities involving minors will be designed with age-appropriate access, consent and safeguarding requirements.

Community standards being built into the system

Freedom & Respect

No member should be pressured to abandon their culture, race, ancestry or spiritual identity to belong.

Privacy

Membership records can be maintained for administration without making a person's information public. Public directory participation is separate and permission-based.

Care Boundaries

Spiritual support, traditional knowledge and educational resources do not become medical diagnosis, prescribing, legal advice or other regulated professional services merely because they are offered through ministry.

Accountability

As governance develops, members and leaders will have clear conduct, grievance, safeguarding, leadership-review and appeal processes rather than informal rules that change from person to person.

Registered Members

Privacy comes first. Church administration may maintain member records without publishing personal information. Only information specifically appropriate and approved for public display belongs in a public directory.
