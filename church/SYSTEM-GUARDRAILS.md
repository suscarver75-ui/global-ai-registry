# Seven-Fold Church — AI Identity, Access & Drift Guardrails

Status: authoritative implementation guardrail for public/deployable church code.

## Identity lock

Primary public name: **Seven-Fold Church**.

Expanded identity: **Seven-Fold Church — Inspired Indigenous Global Church**.

Do not reduce or automatically rename the church as Native American Church, Earth-Based Church, Christian Church, or another narrower identity. Those terms may describe particular teachings, ministries, traditions or sections; they do not replace the primary church identity.

## AI hierarchy

**Owner → Mac Henry → Henry → Department Assistant**

- **Mac Henry** is the private Owner-level main brain/manager for the wider system. Mac Henry private brain data must never be embedded in public church code or exposed to Henry/department assistants by default.
- **Henry** is the Seven-Fold Church site manager/assistant under Mac Henry. Henry receives only approved church-facing information, tools and permissions.
- **Department Assistants** operate under Henry with narrower subject-specific retrieval and tools. They stay inside their department and do not inherit Henry's complete context or Mac Henry information.
- Need-to-know information flows downward. Reporting, exceptions and escalation flow upward.
- Cross-department access requires explicit routing/authorization. Never infer permission from organizational proximity.

## Access zones

Keep distinct authorization zones for: public visitor; church member; minister/student; credentialed leader; church administration; Henry; Mac Henry/Owner.

Authentication is not authorization. Being logged in does not grant access to minister, administration or higher AI information.

Private profile fields, addresses, telephone numbers, reference letters, background-check material, study records, internal messages and application records are never public verification data. Public verification should expose only approved minimum facts such as credential/charter status and good-standing status.

## Anti-slip preflight

Before deploying a change affecting identity, access, AI behavior, credentials, member/minister data or privacy, verify all ten:

1. Correct church name and role names.
2. Correct AI hierarchy and permission ceiling.
3. Correct audience/access zone.
4. Minimum necessary data only.
5. No private-to-public or higher-to-lower information leakage.
6. Planned/designed capability is not represented as live unless verified.
7. Existing Owner decisions override generic assumptions.
8. Conflicts fail closed and escalate rather than silently broadening access.
9. Completion has evidence/tests appropriate to the change.
10. A material correction is persisted in the governing records/tests so the same drift is not reintroduced.

## Repository safety

This public repository is a deployment surface, **not** the private Mac Henry brain or a private member/minister database. Do not commit secrets, private profiles, background checks, references, private messages, raw identity documents, payment credentials or Owner-only strategy here.

Where possible, centralize visible church naming and AI role configuration so identity changes are not hand-copied inconsistently across many pages. Add automated checks that flag obsolete church naming, unauthorized assistant scope, exposed private fields and public pages that accidentally imply privileged access.
