Christian Ministry School • Leadership Formation

Lead by serving. Teach by living it.

A structured Christian leadership and discipleship pathway built around vision, Scripture, service, mentoring, wise leadership selection, healthy church community, prayer and multiplication of mature disciples.

Leadership Track

Core leadership studies

Vision & Calling

Develop a clear prayerful vision for personal ministry and church service; identify gifts, calling, purpose, community needs and measurable ministry priorities.

Key study: Habakkuk 2:2; Proverbs 29:18; Ephesians 2:10; 4:16; 5:15–17; Romans 12:3–8.

Mentoring & Multiplication

Leadership is not only doing ministry; it is preparing other people to serve, mature, teach and eventually mentor others.

Key study: 2 Timothy 2:1–2; Matthew 28:19–20; 1 Corinthians 4:15–17.

Appointing Leaders Wisely

Do not rush leadership appointments. Examine service, faithfulness, character, teachability, relational maturity, boundaries and readiness before assigning authority.

Key study: 1 Timothy 5:22; Luke 16:12; Matthew 20:25–28.

Small Groups & Community

Study how smaller circles can support discipleship, prayer, relationship, pastoral awareness, learning, accountability and development of future leaders.

Leadership by Example

A leader's teaching and conduct should agree. Students examine integrity, humility, service, stewardship, treatment of people and responsible use of authority.

Discipleship Track

Seven core values of mature discipleship

Love God

Prayer, Scripture, worship, obedience and a life centered on relationship with God. Mark 12:30; Psalm 1:1–3; John 4:23–24.

Love People

Compassion, service, encouragement, practical help, grace and respect for people of every background. Mark 12:31; Philippians 2:3–4.

Live a Godly Life

Character, integrity, spiritual growth, responsible choices and a life that reflects the faith being taught.

Share the Faith

Learn to communicate Christian hope respectfully and clearly while honoring the dignity, conscience and freedom of other people.

Love God's Family

Build up fellow believers, strengthen people who are struggling and create communities marked by care rather than status.

Seek God's Kingdom

Place spiritual purpose, justice, service and eternal values above prestige and material self-interest.

Practice Generosity

Develop generosity of time, service, encouragement and resources without pressure, manipulation or promises of guaranteed financial return.

Mentor Formation

The disciple-making cycle

Gather

Invite learners into relationship, study and service. Identify willingness and potential without promising position or title.

Model

Demonstrate prayer, service, teaching, integrity, preparation and care. A learner should see the conduct expected of a leader.

Mentor

Give supervised responsibility, observe, listen to reports and coach. Feedback asks: What went well? What can improve? What should happen next time?

Mobilize

After preparation and review, release qualified people into appropriate service while maintaining accountability and continuing education.

Healthy mentoring:

Encourage, nurture, resource, invest, coach and stay present. Coaching advises without manipulating and leads without controlling. Spiritual authority is never permission for coercion, humiliation, financial exploitation or abuse.

Course Expansion

Discipleship manual studies

The Christian Academy curriculum will also include building life on Scripture, overcoming faith, God's heart for the lost, the call to serve, Kingdom teaching, loving and serving others, church-leadership foundations, prayer and attributes of a healthy church. These subjects can become individual lessons, Bible-study sessions, reflection assignments and supervised ministry exercises rather than one oversized reading document.

Assessment

What a ministry student demonstrates

Knowledge

Understands assigned Scripture, leadership principles, church responsibilities and ethical boundaries.

Character & Service

Shows reliability, humility, respect, willingness to serve, appropriate boundaries and ability to receive correction.

Practical Formation

Completes supervised teaching, service, group leadership or ministry exercises appropriate to the student's pathway.

Reflection & Review

Reports what was done and taught, receives feedback, identifies improvements and continues formation before additional authority is granted.
