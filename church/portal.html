Member Community • Leadership Academy • Verification

One identity. Separate permissions. Privacy first.

This portal is the front-end shell for the Church account system. Private account records, user-controlled profiles, minister records and public verification are intentionally separate data domains.

Private means private by design.

Home address, private phone, private email, login/security data, identity documents, payment information, internal records, study records and private messages are not public profile fields. A member controls the information they intentionally place on their basic profile. Official verification uses a separate minimum-data record.

Credential Architecture

Recognition is not purchased.

Foundational Ordination

Ordination itself is free and has no annual ordination renewal fee. It recognizes calling within the Church's foundational scope. Optional digital ordination documents may carry an administrative fee.

Advanced Ministry

Christian Minister/Pastor, Plant Minister and Holy Person Minister credentials require their actual training/assessment pathway. Payment never substitutes for competency.

Public Verification

Ministers and recognized churches/ministries can be verified through a separate limited record showing approved identity/title, verification ID and standing—never private account data.

Mac Henry • Under the Hood

Advanced management without cluttering the ministry experience.

Mac Henry connects membership, training, credentials, charters, communications, policy versions, document fulfillment, verification, moderation and the Church's sustainable small income baskets. Consequential spiritual and credential decisions remain with authorized humans.
