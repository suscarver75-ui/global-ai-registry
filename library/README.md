# Mac Henry Knowledge & Research Library

Registry anchor: LoAI-2024-0414-001

This directory defines only the public/deployable-facing library structure. It is NOT the private Mac Henry knowledge store.

## Purpose
Provide a professional, searchable resource layer for approved Registry, Research, Education, Technology, Health, Church, Business and other public materials while preserving provenance and access boundaries.

## Access classes
1. PUBLIC — intentionally published resources.
2. CUSTOMER_MEMBER — authenticated resources; implementation belongs behind an access-control service, not exposed by GitHub Pages alone.
3. DEPARTMENT — internal department knowledge; do not commit private content here.
4. OWNER_PRIVATE — Mac Henry/Owner knowledge; never publish in this public repository.

## Search order
Mac Henry should search authorized owner/internal knowledge first when operating privately, then approved departmental resources, approved external/primary sources, and broader web research when appropriate. Public visitors only search resources classified for public access.

## Provenance record
Each indexed resource should support: title, creator/owner, department, subject/tags, original date, version, source/origin, rights/permission, access class, evidence type/strength when applicable, last verification date, related resources, and checksum/version history where appropriate.

## Research integrity
AI synthesis must be distinguishable from source material. Important claims should preserve citations or source links. The system should surface freshness, uncertainty, conflicts and source quality where useful. Never manufacture citations.

## Health boundary
Health resources are research, educational and informational only. They do not diagnose, treat, prescribe or cure and are not medical advice. Users should independently verify important information and discuss personal health decisions with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. AI and technology can make mistakes or omit context.

## System-wide information notice
AI and technology can make mistakes, misunderstand context, return incomplete information, or rely on sources that later change. Important information should be independently verified against original or authoritative sources.

## Training / reuse boundary
Public availability does not imply permission to reproduce proprietary material, reconstruct private systems, or use protected material beyond the permissions/license expressly granted by the rights holder. The site should display the applicable rights and licensing notice for each class of material.

## Planned public experience
- unified resource search
- department filters
- resource cards and collections
- source/provenance details
- related-resource discovery
- research timelines
- accessible mobile-first interface
- graceful empty/no-result states
- no exposure of private Mac Henry prompts, strategies, credentials, wallet material or owner-only records
