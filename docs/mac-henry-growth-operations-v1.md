# MAC HENRY — OWNER-CONTROLLED GROWTH & OPERATIONS PROGRAM
Status: implementation roadmap and policy contract; NOT evidence of deployed services.
Authority: Owner → private Mac Henry Main Brain → Chief of Staff → scoped managers → scoped workers. One system, distinct trust domains.

## Ten light-years-ahead operating rules
1. Human leads, AI assists; Owner controls money, identity, access, publication and irreversible actions.
2. Control before automation: every job has scope, preflight, budget, idempotency key, approval class and postflight receipt.
3. Protect ownership: own domains, first-party subscriber exports, first-party content rights, portable data and replaceable infrastructure.
4. Ethics by design: truthful claims, disclosed affiliate compensation, opt-in outreach, accessible design, clear cancellation and no impersonation.
5. No runaway loops: rate caps, concurrency limits, kill switch, retries with backoff, dead-letter queue and circuit breakers.
6. Clear output: Chief of Staff reports results, exceptions, money in/out, next decision and verification evidence.
7. Multi-platform distribution, single canonical owned content origin; third-party social accounts are discovery channels.
8. Track real money: impressions → qualified visits → consented leads → meetings → attributed orders → settled commissions → refunds.
9. Simple interfaces, advanced internals: owner dashboard, role-specific employee/vendor views and audit trails.
10. Sustainable growth: cohort economics, privacy, reputation, vendor risk, compliance, reserve and recoverability.

## Additional advancement controls
11. Multi-agent compartmentalization: least privilege, no private Brain to public worker, no shared root keys.
12. Evidence provenance: source URLs, publish date, claims review, conflicts, corrections and editorial review before "Truth They Won't Print" goes public.
13. Defensive AI agents: no untrusted prompt can grant permissions, tool arguments policy-checked, secrets never in prompts/logs, isolated build runners and outbound allowlists.
14. Reliability: health probes, signed releases, tested backup/restore, rollbacks, error budgets and owner alerts.
15. Continuous improvement: experiment registry, holdout measurement, consent-aware analytics, human review of failed conversions and monthly security reviews.
16. Model/provider independence: interchangeable LLM adapters; deterministic workflow engine owns state and authorization.
17. First-party audience portability: export subscriptions and content; opt-in only, unsubscribe and deletion honored.
18. Verifiable commerce: offer inventory, partner approvals, disclosure per promotion, signed conversion callbacks, refund-aware attribution.
19. Financial guardrails: no custody or deposit-taking, no guaranteed returns, no insider information trading, regulated activity reviewed by licensed experts.
20. Future-ready R&D: quantum-resistant cryptography migration plan based on standards, never pretend current infrastructure has quantum capabilities.

## Department operating contracts
### 1. AI influencer and investigative editorial
Editorial intake → primary-source research → fact-check → risk/legal review → approved script → original media → captions/alt text → platform formatting → publication → attribution → correction workflow. Label opinion, hypothesis, advertisement and verified reporting distinctly. No deepfake impersonation, fake testimonials or invented investigations.

### 2. Own-domain vertical feed and microdramas
First-party mobile-first full-screen feed; video CDN, HLS/adaptive streaming, accessibility captions, metadata, playlist scheduler, optional product stickers, verified offer pages, embedded feed. Authenticated likes/comments need anti-spam, moderation, abuse reporting, content retention and privacy policy. No invented engagement. Embed must be isolated and constrained by CSP. A drama app can reuse media catalog, rights ledger, episodes, subscriber permissions and analytics.

### 3. Affiliate conversion engine
Owner-approved affiliate link → validate domain/partner terms → offer disclosure and claims checks → compliant landing page → opt-in form → double opt-in where required → CRM lead → source/consent timestamp → AI FAQ grounded in offer docs → email/SMS only with channel-specific consent → booked conversation or merchant checkout → webhook verification → refund/chargeback adjustments → commission reconciliation. Never fabricate leads, claim a sale without a merchant receipt or promise autonomous closing before integrations work. No scraping private data or spam.

### 4. Client and business-network acquisition
Permissioned prospect discovery from public and licensed sources; enrich with provenance; qualification; human-reviewed outreach; CRM; meetings; proposals; contract signature; invoice; paid status. Referral commissions only under written agreements and applicable licensing rules. Track cost per qualified lead, win rate, gross margin and cash collected.

### 5. Digital signage squad
Owner controls tenancy, campaigns, screen groups, employee/vendor assignments, content rights and approval. Players use short-lived scoped tokens, signed manifests, encrypted transport, local offline cache, expiry, emergency override, remote revoke, screen heartbeat, device attestation where available and proof-of-play. Vendors cannot view owner Brain or other tenants. No secret API keys in signage clients.

### 6. Real-estate intelligence (later-stage regulated lane)
Discover off-market brokerage acquisition opportunities through licensed/public data, consented owner outreach, company registry, business-sale listings, broker partnerships and diligence. Record data provenance, seller authorization, conflicts, financial diligence and legal review. Real-estate referrals, brokerage activities and transaction-based fees require jurisdiction-specific licensing review; do not execute unlicensed brokerage or imply private access to listings.

### 7. Contracts and market intelligence
Procurement opportunities from official portals and authorized feeds; filter for eligibility, budget, deadlines, competition, margin and bid/no-bid; verify official documents. $50K+ is a search threshold, not guaranteed contract value or win. Maintain alerts and deadlines only after a real scheduler and source connectors are configured.

### 8. Financial institution context and money intelligence
Explain fee structures, settlement rails, credit, payment risks and public institutional incentives using cited records. Consent-based financial data connectors are read-only by default; no credential collection by agents. Separate educational content from financial advice. No 'insider' trading or misuse of nonpublic information. Banking, custody, investment advice and securities activity require appropriate authorization.

### 9. AirNode / MEGA BASE sovereign infrastructure
Self-owned control plane: identity, authorization, event bus, durable job queue, consent store, content registry, lead CRM, policy engine, secret vault, observability, audit receipts and backup/recovery. Own domain/DNS and portable configurations; external hosting, registrars, email, payment rails and telecom still require contracted providers until independently operated and verified. Quantum roadmap: crypto inventory, agile key rotation, NIST-standard post-quantum migration when supported; do not market speculative quantum revenue.

## Job lifecycle
SCAN → RECONCILE → PROTECT → PRIORITIZE → ROUTE → PREFLIGHT → EXECUTE OR HOLD → VERIFY → RECORD → REPORT → LEARN.
Each job must have job_id, tenant_id, actor, capability, approved scope, data classification, budget, source provenance, policy decision, idempotency key, retry policy, receipt, final verified state and owner-facing exception.

Never show simulated metrics as real.

SPECIFIED → EXECUTABLE → TESTED → INTEGRATED → REAL SERVICE CONNECTED → END-TO-END EXERCISED → MONITORED & RECOVERABLE → VERIFIED OPERATIONAL.
