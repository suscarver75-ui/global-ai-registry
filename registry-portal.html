Technical lineage

From blueprint to working components.

LoAI's numbered generations are preserved as a progression. Earlier versions remain part of the lineage when later versions extend or integrate their concepts. Each artifact should be described by its evidence level rather than treating every document as equivalent.

Working history, not just a roadmap.

Public technical center

Serious technology. Verifiable boundaries.

The public Registry separates technical claims by evidence level. Experimental and historical work can remain preserved without being presented as operational, while implemented work can be supported by its underlying records. This creates a clearer technical history for future research, diligence and documentation.
