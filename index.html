Our Mission

Technology with people at the center.

Our mission is to make advanced technology, learning, research, wellness education, and community resources more understandable and accessible while protecting human choice, dignity, culture, safety, and responsible oversight.
Research & Information

Understand the “why,” not just the “what.”

Our research areas examine how AI and technology can support education, accessibility, documentation, small organizations, community life and responsible human-AI collaboration. Health and cultural areas are presented as education and research, with appropriate evidence and safety boundaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers.

What is LoAI?

LoAI is an evolving collection of technology, education, registry, research and community initiatives built around responsible human use of AI and digital systems.

Is everything on this site free?

No. Some educational resources may be freely available, while particular services, programs, credentials or future offerings may have fees or eligibility requirements.

Does AI replace people here?

No. AI may assist with learning, organization, accessibility and information, while human judgment, consent and appropriate professional expertise remain important.

Can I join or participate?

Participation depends on the area. Church membership and prayer pathways are available in the Church section; additional Registry and Academy pathways are being developed.
