Coming Forward • Academy

LoAI Academy — Multiple Learning Branches

The Academy is being developed as a broader education system, not only an AI school. Different branches will support technology learning, ministry preparation, Bible and faith studies, practical education and continuing learning.

TECHNOLOGY BRANCH

AI & Digital Empowerment

AI literacy, responsible technology, accessibility, business skills, research literacy, ethics, documentation and practical human-AI collaboration.

MINISTRY BRANCH

Minister Training & Formation

Structured church-ministry education, pastoral responsibility, ethics, community service, spiritual leadership and preparation connected to the church's ministry pathway.

FAITH STUDIES

Bible & Scripture Courses

Bible study, scripture exploration, spiritual history, comparative learning, guided study courses and resources for individual and group learning.

ORDINATION PATHWAY

Ordination Preparation

A church-governed educational pathway for candidates pursuing ordination, including required study, ministry preparation and review. Completion of coursework alone will not automatically represent ordination.

COMMUNITY LEARNING

Life, Service & Continuing Education

Practical courses and educational resources that can grow across community service, leadership, accessibility, research and lifelong learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers.

What is LoAI?

LoAI brings together technology, education, registry, research and community initiatives built around responsible human use of AI and digital systems.

Is everything free?

No. Some resources may be freely available while services, programs, courses, credentials or other offerings may have fees or eligibility requirements.

What will the Academy teach?

The planned Academy includes AI and digital education as well as ministry training, Bible and scripture studies, ordination preparation, community learning and continuing education.

Does completing an Academy course make someone an ordained minister?

No. Ordination is a separate church-governed process with its own requirements, review and authorization.
