The original series

Beauty. Desire. Survival. Ancestral Truth.

Before the Great Dividing, the First World lived in balance. Now a hidden descendant of Earth Chief JohnAnn is drawn into a dangerous Convergence, caught between a fierce warrior of Fire and a captivating prince of Water. Their rivalries uncover secret children, lost family lines, betrayals and the technology that remembers what the nations were taught to forget.

👑

Earth Chief JohnAnn

The Original Blood Earth Mother. Beautiful, formidable and wise, she draws on every element. Her choices during the Great Dividing still shape the fate of her descendants.

🌿

Aeryn of Terranova

JohnAnn's direct descendant, unaware of her full inheritance. She must survive a struggle for her bloodline without surrendering her freedom to choose whom she loves.

🔥

Kael of Ignara

A warrior whose loyalty is tested by jealousy, desire, and a secret from his past.

💧

Thalen of Aquaria

A prince whose tenderness hides a family promise—and a reason he sought Aeryn in the first place.
