The story begins

Before the nations divided, they were one.

Earth Chief JohnAnn, the beautiful and wise matriarch of the original bloodline, holds a connection to every element. Generations later, her descendant Aeryn becomes entangled with Kael of the Fire Nation and Thalen of the Water Nation. Jealousy, hidden children, survival trials, and an ancient secret threaten the fragile peace. The lands themselves are alive: families draw strength from their rivers, forests, storms, light and fire.

The ancestral center

The Ancestral Crown

Once the heart of a united civilization, the Crown holds the Hall of Ancestors, the Memory Gardens, and the gates between worlds. When its ancient systems awaken, the truth of the Great Dividing begins to emerge.
