# THE ELEMENTAL WORLD — SERIES BIBLE v0.1
Mac Henry Studios · Creative development · Not a published episode

## Logline
In seven breathtaking civilizations powered by their own living lands, Aeryn—a descendant of the Original Blood Earth Mother, Chief JohnAnn—becomes the focus of a forbidden romance between Kael of Fire and Thalen of Water. When a child erased from the royal records resurfaces, the ancient Technology World, Nexara, reveals a truth that could reunite or destroy the divided nations.

## Visual production rules
- Photorealistic adult performers, grounded human movement and natural dialogue; beautiful fantasy hints rather than cartoon effects.
- Nature is a character: rich emerald forests, turquoise rivers, coral reefs, copper volcanic gardens, lavender sky gardens, golden luminous groves, electric crystal streams.
- Elemental power begins in daily life: cooking, building, raising children, medicine as fiction, tending gardens, community rituals, navigation, and celebrations. Power is sourced from healthy relationships with the land; overuse has ecological consequences.
- Each nation has distinctive color, architecture, textiles, wildlife, music, and family customs. Avoid generic interchangeable cultures.
- Mature romantic tension, sensuality, adult relationships, jealousy, secrets, grief and reconciliation. All romantic characters are adults. Children appear only in nonsexual family/mystery plots.
- Original visual language, creatures and plots; cinematic emotional scope without copying existing franchises.

## Canon characters
- **Earth Chief JohnAnn**: original bloodline matriarch, mature beauty, deep wisdom, complex history; can work with Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Energy, Light, and Nexara's ancient intelligent technology. Powers have costs. Photo reference supplied by owner in conversation; not copied into public repository without explicit publication approval.
- **Aeryn of Terranova**: direct descendant of JohnAnn; raised with ancestral teachings; her gifts awaken across elemental boundaries. Independent and capable of choosing her relationships.
- **Kael of Ignara**: Fire warrior, passionate, proud, jealous; learns that love is not possession.
- **Thalen of Aquaria**: Water prince, charming and attentive; his family's promise to JohnAnn creates a concealed duty. His affection becomes genuine.
- **The Lost Child**: an heir hidden during political conflict; a non-romantic mystery character whose safety matters more than dynastic ambitions.
- **Nexaran Guardian**: intelligent technological being holding fragmentary records of the First World.

## World geography and family life
- **Terranova / Earth**: living tree homes, gardens, forest keepers, root listening, land stewardship.
- **Aquaria / Water**: reef settlements, waterways, current navigation, ancestral songs in tide pools.
- **Ignara / Fire**: volcanic gardens, forging, cooking hearths, community fire celebrations.
- **Zephyria / Air**: floating villages, wind-powered homes, cloud orchards, gliding.
- **Voltara / Energy**: crystalline power gardens, safe energy crafts, invention.
- **Lumoria / Light**: golden groves, light-carried art and archives, remembrance.
- **Nexara / Technology**: intelligent architecture, synthetic beings, responsive gardens, recovered memories.
- **Ancestral Crown**: common origin, Hall of Ancestors, Memory Gardens, Chamber of Origins and the gateways.

## Historical spine
The First World joined seven ancestral houses. The Great Dividing combined a real environmental catastrophe, difficult choices, political betrayal and altered records. Every nation inherited an incomplete account. JohnAnn knows a deeper truth but is not infallible. Her ancestral family line and the lost child's history become crucial to recovering what happened.

## Season 1 — 8 episode beats
1. **The Garden Answers** — Aeryn's garden changes colors during a family celebration. Kael arrives with orders he refuses to explain. End: water rises at Aeryn's feet though no Aquarian is nearby.
2. **A Prince in the Rain** — Thalen protects Aeryn during a storm. Kael sees their intimacy. End: Thalen recognizes JohnAnn's ancestral mark.
3. **The Convergence** — Representatives gather for a ceremony that becomes a survival trial. End: Nexaran systems announce an unauthorized bloodline.
4. **The Fire Between Us** — Kael and Aeryn share an intimate confession; Thalen reveals he knows where her father disappeared. End: Aeryn discovers a portrait of a woman identical to her.
5. **What the Water Kept** — Aquarian archives expose a forbidden marriage. Kael discovers evidence of a child he was told had died. End: the child calls him father.
6. **The Price of Protection** — Thalen's original mission comes to light; Aeryn must decide whom to trust. End: the child is taken toward Nexara.
7. **The Mother Returns** — JohnAnn intervenes as all seven forces destabilize; a Nexaran Guardian recognizes her. End: the Guardian says JohnAnn once ordered the archives sealed.
8. **The Gates Remember** — Aeryn discovers why the archives were sealed, saves the child, and rejects the Convergence's false choice. JohnAnn opens the gates. End: the world invites new residents to enter.

## Pilot cold open — screenwriting excerpt
EXT. TERRANOVA — ANCIENT FAMILY GARDEN — DAWN

Sunlight catches dew on enormous emerald leaves. A mother prepares breakfast over a warm stone hearth. Two children race along a moss-covered bridge, followed by a tiny bird with faintly luminous wings.

AERYN (adult) kneels beside her GRANDMOTHER, tending a stubborn silver-blue flower.

GRANDMOTHER
You can't make it open by demanding it.

AERYN
I've been asking nicely for an hour.

GRANDMOTHER
Then listen for an hour.

Aeryn closes her eyes. Beneath the garden, roots pulse with gentle golden light. The flower opens—then changes from blue to crimson.

The grandmother's smile vanishes.

GRANDMOTHER
Where did you learn to call fire?

A distant horn sounds. Beyond the flowering trees, a rider approaches through rising ash.

CUT TO TITLE: BLOOD OF THE ANCESTORS.
